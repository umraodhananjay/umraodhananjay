## 💫 About Me

I am a **Project Executive Officer at IIT Kanpur**, leading development on the **Virtual Labs** initiative — a national programme that brings hands-on laboratory experience to students who may never step into a physical lab.

My work sits at the intersection of **engineering, education and emerging technology**:

- 🔬 I **design and build browser-based simulations** that turn abstract engineering and science concepts into something students can actually touch, break and retry.
- 🥽 I develop **Virtual Reality based experiments**, extending traditional lab work into immersive 3D environments.
- 👨‍🏫 I **train and mentor faculty and students** across institutions to build their own simulators — from concept design to deployment.
- 💻 As a **senior software developer**, I have shipped multiple production software products and websites over the years.
- 🤖 I am actively exploring **AI and new technologies**, and I'm always open to collaboration.

> 🎯 **Mission:** make quality lab education accessible to every student, anywhere, on any device.
